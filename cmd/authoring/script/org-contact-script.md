# Contacting an org before we list it (ADR-029)

For the editor. Use the words below as written. Open an org's row on the Orgs page and this script fills in its name, places, and what we would say. Fill in [number] yourself. Log every email and call on that row.

Only a clear yes lists an org. No reply is not a yes.

## Before you start

Open the org's row and note:

- the places where it appears
- its clicks in the last 30 days
- "What we would say": the statements we would show about it

Clicks count people who opened the org's website or tapped its phone number from our pages. They do not count calls. If the org is not listed yet, its clicks are 0; use the number for the public orgs in the same place to give a fair estimate.

## Email

Subject: May we list [Org name] on RenterLaw?

Hello,

My name is [your name]. I work with RenterLaw (renterlaw.org), a website that explains renter law in plain words. Every statement on the site links to the law or the official page it comes from.

We have a page called "Local Help" for [place]. It lists places a renter can go for help. We would like to include [Org name]. This is what we would say:

[paste the statements, exactly as they appear]

Before we list you, we want to be sure you can take the extra contacts. About [number] people a month open the Local Help page for [place]. We cannot predict how many would contact you.

Could you tell us:

1. May we list you?
2. Is the information above correct?
3. How would you like renters to reach you? For example a phone number, email, intake form, or walk-in hours.

If you say no, we will not list you. If you say yes and later change your mind, reply to this email or use renterlaw.org/contact, and we will take the listing down within two business days.

RenterLaw is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. We do not charge the groups we list.

Thank you,
[your name]
RenterLaw

## Call

Ask for the person who handles intake or outreach.

"Hi, my name is [your name]. I'm calling from RenterLaw, a website that explains renter law in plain words. We have a Local Help page for [place], and we'd like to list [Org name] on it. Before we do, we want to make sure you can handle the extra contacts. Is there someone I can speak with about that?"

When you reach the right person:

"About [number] people a month open our Local Help page for [place]. We'd show your name, what you help with, and how to reach you. Can I read you what we'd say?"

Read the statements. Then ask:

1. "May we list you?"
2. "Is everything I read correct?"
3. "How would you like renters to reach you? A phone number, email, intake form, walk-in hours?"

If they ask how to change or remove it later: "Email us or use renterlaw.org/contact, and we'll take it down within two business days."

If they want to think about it: "Of course. When would be a good time for me to follow up?" Log a "maybe" and set that date.

If you leave a message: say your name, RenterLaw, that you would like to list them on a renter-help website and want their OK first, and your call-back number. Log "left message".

## Logging it

Open the org's row. Each question has its own field.

**1. May we list you?** Pick what happened:

| What happened | Pick |
|---|---|
| Nobody answered | No answer |
| You left a voicemail or sent the email | Left message |
| They want to think, ask someone, or see more | Maybe (set a follow-up date) |
| They said yes | Yes, list us |
| They said no | No, leave us off |

**2. Is the information correct?** Pick Yes or Needs changes. For Needs changes, write exactly what to change.

**3. How would you like renters to reach you?** Under "Their contacts", add each one they give you: a phone, email, website, intake form, text line, address, or walk-in hours. Give it a short label if it helps (for example "Intake line, Mon to Fri 9 to 5"). Tick "preferred" on the ones they want renters to use first.

Write who you spoke with and anything else they said in the note. A changed number or page reaches the site only after a person edits the statement.
