# Topic map

The one piece of context every drafting and review agent gets beyond its own page. Keep it to this size.

Two surfaces, two jobs (ADR-026):

- A **playbook** answers one *situation* a renter is in, as steps in the order they act. Its title is the renter's search question.
- A **concept** answers one *informational question* ("how much can my landlord charge for a deposit?"). Concept pages are assembled later from tagged statements across places.

A statement is one informative claim, tagged with the concept whose question it answers. It sits on a playbook only if a renter in that situation needs it to act. Otherwise it belongs to another playbook, or it is parked for its concept page. Taking a statement off a playbook never deletes it.

## Playbooks: situation, what it covers, what goes elsewhere

| Topic | Situation question | Covers | Not here → goes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| cant-pay-rent | "I can't pay my rent. What can I do?" | talking to the landlord, payment plans, partial payments, late fees and grace periods, rent help programs, rent debt collection | any notice to quit, pay-or-quit notice, court step → **eviction-defense** |
| eviction-defense | "I got an eviction notice or court papers. What happens now?" | notices (pay-or-quit included), paying to stop the case, the court case, answering, the hearing, judgment, removal by the sheriff or officer, appeal, eviction records | lockouts, shutoffs or removal without a court → **landlord-entry** (lockout) or **renting-fundamentals** |
| security-deposits | "My landlord didn't return my deposit. What can I do?" | move-out, the return deadline, the itemized list, deductions, what you can sue for | how much a landlord can charge, deposit receipts, escrow and interest → **move-in-checklist** or parked for the concept |
| move-in-checklist | "I'm moving in. What should I check and what can I be charged?" | deposit caps, receipts, escrow and interest, move-in inspection and photos, the lease basics to check | anything at move-out → **security-deposits** / **move-out-checklist** |
| move-out-checklist | "I'm moving out. How do I protect my deposit?" | notice to end the lease, the move-out inspection, photos, forwarding address | suing for the deposit → **security-deposits** |
| repairs-and-habitability | "My landlord won't fix something. What can I do?" | what must be fixed, asking in writing, records, inspections (with the condemnation caution), withholding, repair and deduct, rent cuts, moving out (each with its risk) | heat-only rules → **heat-not-working**; the eviction case itself → **eviction-defense** |
| heat-not-working | "My heat doesn't work. What can I do?" | heat season and temperatures, reporting, emergency steps, utility shutoffs, energy help | general repair remedies → **repairs-and-habitability** |
| landlord-entry | "Can my landlord come in? What about my locks?" | notice to enter, allowed reasons, emergencies, lock changes, lockouts, quiet enjoyment | eviction through court → **eviction-defense** |
| rent-increase | "My landlord raised my rent. Is that allowed?" | notice periods, caps and rent control, increases during a lease, retaliatory increases | being told to leave at the end of a lease → **eviction-defense** / **lease-renewal** |
| constructive-eviction | "My home is so bad I have to leave. Do I still owe rent?" | when conditions let you leave, the steps first, the risk | fixing the problem while staying → **repairs-and-habitability** |
| resource-directory | "Where can I get help?" | one organisation per entry: who it helps, how to reach it | rules of any kind → the topic they belong to |

Cross-cutting rules (renting-fundamentals concepts: retaliation, court-eviction-only, illegal-lockout, records-and-evidence, fair-housing, mediation, small-claims-court, complaint-line) may appear on any playbook, but only where that situation needs them.

## Concepts: the question each one answers

Tag a statement with the concept whose question it answers.

- **cant-pay-rent**
  - grace-period: Is there a grace period before rent is late?
  - late-fees: How much can my landlord charge for late rent?
  - late-rent-notice: What notice must my landlord give when rent is late?
  - partial-payments: Can I pay part of my rent?
  - rent-assistance-programs: Where can I get help paying rent?
  - rent-debt-collection: What happens to rent I still owe?
- **eviction-defense**
  - notice-to-quit: How much notice must I get before an eviction case?
  - answer-the-case: How do I respond to an eviction case?
  - eviction-court-process: What happens in eviction court?
  - pay-and-stay: Can I pay and stop an eviction?
  - eviction-record: Will an eviction stay on my record?
- **security-deposits**
  - deposit-cap: How much can my landlord charge for a deposit?
  - deposit-receipt: Do I get a receipt for my deposit?
  - deposit-escrow-interest: Does my deposit earn interest, and where is it kept?
  - deposit-return-deadline: How long does my landlord have to return my deposit?
  - deduction-itemization: What can my landlord take out of my deposit?
  - deposit-damages: What can I get if my landlord keeps my deposit unfairly?
- **repairs-and-habitability**
  - habitability-standard: What must my landlord keep in working order?
  - repair-request-in-writing: How do I ask for repairs?
  - code-inspection: Who can inspect my home?
  - rent-withholding: Can I stop paying rent until repairs are made?
  - repair-and-deduct: Can I fix it myself and take it off the rent?
- **heat-not-working**
  - utility-shutoff-protection: Can my utilities be shut off?
- **landlord-entry**
  - entry-notice-period: How much notice must my landlord give to enter?
  - entry-allowed-reasons: When can my landlord enter?
  - emergency-entry: Can my landlord enter in an emergency?
  - entry-penalties: What if my landlord enters illegally?
  - lock-change-rules: Can my landlord change my locks?
  - quiet-enjoyment: What is my right to quiet enjoyment?
- **rent-increase**
  - increase-notice-period: How much notice must I get for a rent increase?
  - mid-lease-protection: Can my rent go up during my lease?
  - rent-control: Is there a limit on rent increases where I live?
- **constructive-eviction**
  - constructive-eviction: Can I leave if my home is unlivable?
- **resource-directory**
  - help-lines: Who can I call for help?
  - legal-aid: Where can I get free legal help?
  - housing-counseling: Where can I get housing counseling?
- **renting-fundamentals**
  - retaliation-protection: Can my landlord punish me for complaining?
  - court-eviction-only: Can my landlord evict me without going to court?
  - illegal-lockout: What if my landlord locks me out?
  - records-and-evidence: What records should I keep?
  - fair-housing: What discrimination is illegal?
  - mediation: Can a mediator help with my landlord?
  - small-claims-court: How do I use small claims court?
  - complaint-line: Where can I file a complaint?
  - federal-housing-assistance: What federal housing help is there?
