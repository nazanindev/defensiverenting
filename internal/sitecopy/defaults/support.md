RenterLaw is free. It has no ads, no affiliate links, and no data to sell. We think tenant rights information should be free no matter what you earn.

The work behind it is not free. We research the law and quote it exactly. We check every answer against where it comes from. We check every source again each week, as laws change. That time deserves fair pay.

## How to help right now

We are still working out how to fund the site. Until then, the most useful thing you can do is share a guide with someone who needs it.

If a guide helped you, or if you want to fund the work, [write to us](/contact). We would like to hear from you.

## Where the money will go

- Fair pay for research and review
- New cities, states, and topics
- Keeping existing guides current as laws change
