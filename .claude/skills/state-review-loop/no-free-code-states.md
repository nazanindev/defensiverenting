# States with no fetchable official code (decided 2026-10-02)

Georgia, Tennessee, Mississippi and Arkansas publish their official code only through Lexis. Indiana's code site (iga.in.gov) blocks software. Never fetch or cite advance.lexis.com, and never cite Justia, FindLaw or any other code mirror.

## What you may cite instead

1. **A government copy.** A government office's own publication that reprints the law or states the rule with its section number: the attorney general, the courts, a state or county agency. Kind `gov_guidance`. It must be on a government domain (.gov, a state domain, a court site) or one listed below. A county page that only uploaded someone else's printout does not count unless it is listed below.
   - Publisher names the office and how current the copy says it is, for example "Mississippi Attorney General (Miss. Code 89-8, as amended through 2018)". If the copy gives no date, write "undated".
2. **A passed law.** The act as passed, on the legislature's own site. Kind `statute`. Quote only words that are law now: never quote struck-out text, and in a PDF where you cannot tell struck from added words, quote only a section that is entirely new.
3. **A court opinion.** Kind `court_ruling`. Use the court's own site. Tennessee court PDFs block software, so for Tennessee only, a courtlistener.com copy of a Tennessee opinion is allowed. Quote the statute words the opinion quotes, and say the court quoted them.

**Newer law check.** Before you quote a government copy, check the newer-law list for your state below. If a later law changed that section, quote the passed law instead, or drop the fact.

## Per-state sources (all fetch clean through fetch_source as of 2026-10-02)

**Georgia**
- Dept. of Community Affairs, Georgia Landlord-Tenant Handbook, revised 8-29-24: https://dca.georgia.gov/document/manuals/georgia-landlord-tenant-handbook-english/download
- Safe at Home Act (HB 404, 2024) as passed: https://gov.georgia.gov/document/2024-signed-legislation/hb-404/download (repairs and fit to live in 44-7-13, cooling as a utility 44-7-14.1, deposit cap 44-7-30.1, 3-business-day pay-or-leave notice 44-7-50; leases entered or renewed on or after 7-1-2024).
- The HB 404 PDF prints a line number at the start of every line, and the numbers sit inside the text. A quote cannot run across a line break: quote the words of one line, or cite the same url twice with one piece each.
- The handbook PDF is two columns; some passages interleave. Quote only passages that read cleanly as one run of text.
- Newer law: a 2026 act changes 44-7-50 from 2027-01-01 (sealing of eviction records). Not in effect yet; do not write it as current law.

**Mississippi**

- Newer law (legislature bill pages; struck text is dropped by the fetcher). The AG copy is OUT OF DATE for every section below: quote these sections from the bill, never from the AG copy.
  - 2022 SB 2461 (as sent to governor): https://billstatus.ls.state.ms.us/documents/2022/html/SB/2400-2499/SB2461SG.htm . Amends 89-8-3, 89-8-7 (definitions), 89-8-9, 89-8-11, 89-8-13 (ending the tenancy, notices), 89-8-15 (repairs), 89-8-17, 89-8-19, and eviction sections 89-7-27 to 89-7-47. Creates the eviction process 89-8-31 to 89-8-45.
  - 2025 SB 2328 (as sent to governor): https://billstatus.ls.state.ms.us/documents/2025/html/SB/2300-2399/SB2328SG.htm . Amends 89-8-3, 89-8-31, 89-8-35, 89-8-39 again: for these four, quote the 2025 bill.
  - 2023 HB 65 (domestic violence lease exit) DIED. It is not law. Never cite it.
  - The AG copy is still fine for sections no bill above touched, such as 89-8-5, 89-8-21 (deposits), 89-8-23, 89-8-25, 89-8-27.
  - In the bill pages, "* * *" marks where struck words were removed. A quote may not span "* * *": quote the words on one side of it.

**Tennessee** (URLTA, T.C.A. 66-28, applies only in 17 counties: Anderson, Blount, Bradley, Davidson, Hamilton, Knox, Madison, Maury, Montgomery, Rutherford, Sevier, Shelby, Sullivan, Sumner, Washington, Williamson, Wilson. Every statement says which counties it covers. The Legal Aid booklets list the 17 counties but never name the act: a statement may say "covered by the landlord-tenant act" only if one of its own quotes names the act (a court opinion quoting 66-28-102 can). Otherwise say "In 17 Tennessee counties (Anderson, ... Wilson)" or "In the 17 counties listed in the Legal Aid Society renter booklet", citing the booklet's county-list line. The TN AG laws page lists 19 counties and is wrong: never cite it for coverage.)
- Renter brochures hosted by the TN Dept. of Health (written by Legal Aid Society; plain language, no section numbers; two-column layout, keep quotes to one line):

  - Other counties: https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/health/documents/healthy-places/healthy-homes/HHW_LAS_TN_Rural_Renters_Rights.pdf (do not repeat its mid-lease rent increase line)
- Smith v. Meek, TN Court of Appeals, 7-28-2025 (quotes 66-28-304(a)(2), 66-28-501(a), 66-28-401, 66-28-516): https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10642837/stacie-smith-v-gary-meek/ (verified; the bare id url returns a 404 page)

- Newer law: Public Chapter 907 of 2024 (from 1-1-2025): landlord must give the manager, an agent for notices, and a phone, email or portal for repairs (66-28-302). The brochures predate it. There is NO Tennessee deposit cap (the 2024 junk fee bill failed).
