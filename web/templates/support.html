Support this project

Every claim on this site cites its primary source, so you can check the law yourself. Making that real is the work: researching the statutes, quoting them exactly, verifying every citation against its source before it's published, and keeping the guides current as the law changes.

Good research takes time. We think that time deserves fair pay.

What your support funds

  • Fair pay for research and verification
  • Research time to expand into new cities and topics
  • Keeping existing guides current as laws change

This site has no ads, no affiliate links, and no data to sell. It exists because we think tenant rights information should be free and accessible regardless of your income.

How to donate

We're setting up transparent, public funding through Open Collective, where every donation and every expense is visible to anyone. We'll share the link here as soon as it's live.

Donation link coming soon.

In the meantime, the most valuable thing you can do is share a guide with someone who needs it.

Transparency

When the Open Collective page is live, you'll be able to see exactly how much has been raised and exactly what it was spent on. No black box. We believe the people who fund this work deserve to know where it goes.
