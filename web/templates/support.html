Support this project

Every guide on this site is written by a person — not generated by AI, not copied from another source. Our writer reads the statutes, finds the relevant sections, and turns dense legal language into something a renter can actually use in a difficult moment.

Good research takes time. We think that time deserves fair pay.

What your support funds

  • Fair wages for our writer
  • Research time to expand into new cities and topics
  • Keeping existing guides current as laws change

This site has no ads, no affiliate links, and no data to sell. It exists because we think tenant rights information should be free and accessible regardless of your income.
