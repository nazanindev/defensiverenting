Cameron Monteith

Reviewing Editor

Cameron Monteith is the reviewing editor for every guide on RenterLaw. Some drafts start as AI-generated research. Others he researches himself. Either way, everything is checked by hand before it goes live: each statement is verified against the primary legal source it cites.

Cameron has worked in nonprofit spaces in Pittsburgh for seven years and counting, ranging from event organizing and government social work to grant writing. He believes in equitable housing and walkable infrastructure. When not volunteering for RenterLaw, he publishes fiction and poetry under the pen name J.B. Kalf.

How the pipeline works

Curious about the machinery? Our drafting system enforces citations at the data level. A citation can only be saved when its quote appears word for word in the text of the fetched source, so a fabricated citation cannot enter the system. Drafts always land in a review queue. Cameron's hand check is the final gate, and nothing is published without it.
