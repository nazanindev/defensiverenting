Who we are

RenterLaw is made by 2 people. Every guide passes through both of us before it goes live.

Cameron Monteith

Reviewing editor

Cameron reviews every guide before it is published. He reads each statement against the source it cites. Some drafts start as software research. Others he researches himself. Either way, nothing goes live until he has checked it by hand.

Cameron has worked in nonprofits in Pittsburgh for 7 years, from event organizing and government social work to grant writing. He believes in fair housing and walkable cities. Outside RenterLaw, he publishes fiction and poetry under the pen name J.B. Kalf.

Nazanin

Engineer

Nazanin is the engineer who built RenterLaw and runs it. She designed the data model and the drafting pipeline, and she reviews guides. She likes bringing software engineering to fields far from software to see what she can build. She believes everyone should be able to find their housing rights.

To read how a guide gets made and checked, see about RenterLaw. To tell us a guide is wrong, use the report form.
